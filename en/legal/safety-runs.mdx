_Last updated: 2026-06-02_

**RulePack AI** — surfaced as **Safety Runs** and the **"Safety checked"** badge —
scans rule files for prompt injection, credential exfiltration, unsafe or
destructive commands, persistence, stealth instructions, and obfuscated
payloads, combining precise static rules with a local model.

## No guarantee

No automated scan — RulePack AI included — can catch every malicious or unsafe
instruction. A passing scan and a "Safety checked" badge mean **no issues were
found by the checks that ran at that time** — they are **not** a guarantee that
a pack is safe, nor a substitute for your own judgment.

You remain responsible for the rule files you install and the agents you run
them in. Always review packs from sources you don't already trust before giving
them to an agent.

## Our ongoing commitment

Attack patterns evolve, so RulePack AI does too. We:

- update the detection rules and model prompts as new attack patterns appear;
- re-scan packs against newer checks over time;
- record every scan so a result can be revisited.

A badge reflects the checks available when that version was scanned; a later
re-scan can change the result as our coverage improves.

## Reporting

Found something a scan missed? Use the **Report** button on the pack page, or
[contact support](/support). See also the
[Acceptable Use Policy](/docs/legal/acceptable-use).
